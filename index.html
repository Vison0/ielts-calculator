FAQ

How is the overall band score calculated?

Average of Listening, Reading, Writing, Speaking, rounded to the nearest half band. E.g. 6.625 → 6.5, 6.75 → 7.0.

Academic vs General reading difference?

Same 0–9 scale, but General Reading needs more correct answers per band (texts are easier). Listening is identical.

How many correct for band 7 Listening?

30–31 out of 40.

How many correct for band 7 Reading?

Academic: 30–32. General: 34–35.
